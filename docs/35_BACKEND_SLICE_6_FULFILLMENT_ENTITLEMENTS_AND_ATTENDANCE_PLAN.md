## 4. The generalized commercial spine — quote invariant first (Correction 1)

**Required quote invariant (owner-stated, adopted verbatim):** a `price_quote` describes exactly one commercial fulfillment shape. New column **`commercial_shape text NOT NULL DEFAULT 'capacityPurchase' CHECK (commercial_shape IN ('capacityPurchase','entitlementAcquisition','entitlementReservation'))`**, and `ck_price_quote_one_unit` is REPLACED by:

**`ck_price_quote_shape`** —
- `capacityPurchase` ⇒ `num_nonnulls(session_id, camp_week_id, cohort_id) = 1` AND `option_kind IN ('dropIn','monthly','term','camp','free')` AND `fulfillment_revision_id IS NULL` AND `entitlement_id IS NULL` — today's shape, byte-identical semantics;
- `entitlementAcquisition` ⇒ `num_nonnulls(…) = 0` AND `option_kind IN ('package','membership')` AND **`fulfillment_revision_id IS NOT NULL`** AND `entitlement_id IS NULL` — the unit-less product quote ("10 massages for AED 800" has no Session/CampWeek/Cohort and gets none: no fake capacity, ever);
- `entitlementReservation` ⇒ `num_nonnulls(…) = 1` AND `option_kind IN ('package','membership')` AND `total_fils = 0` AND `fulfillment_revision_id IS NULL` AND **`entitlement_id IS NOT NULL`** — the S6-3 zero-priced occurrence reservation is BOUND TO THE EXACT PURCHASED ENTITLEMENT (Correction A): Ahmed reserving Wednesday 18:00 against `E1` produces a quote anchored to `E1`, never a generic zero-price quote that merely happens to describe Pilates.

New columns/FKs: `fulfillment_revision_id uuid` + composite FK `(fulfillment_revision_id, price_option_id) → price_option_fulfillment_revision (id, price_option_id)` — an acquisition quote is structurally bound to the exact Program, price option/Offer, participant, provider organization (all existing NOT NULL columns), AND the immutable fulfillment terms in force when the customer was quoted; a provider edit between quote and confirmation supersedes the revision but NEVER changes what this quote sells. **`entitlement_id uuid NULL`** + five-column composite FK **`(entitlement_id, account_id, participant_id, organization_id, program_id) → entitlement (id, account_id, participant_id, organization_id, program_id)`** (target unique `uq_entitlement_reservation_lineage` on `entitlement`) — a reservation quote's account ownership, beneficiary participant, provider organization, and Program lineage are provably the ENTITLEMENT'S at the row level; a foreign or mismatched entitlement id cannot produce a quote. **Reservation eligibility derives from the entitlement's purchased immutable revision (Correction A1):** the reservation branch of `requestQuote` reads terms through `entitlement.fulfillment_revision_id` — the purchased snapshot (schedule terms, branch limitation, reservation permission), NEVER the provider's currently-active product configuration; a provider who moves the public product from Mon/Wed to Tue/Thu does not silently mutate an existing customer's entitlement (an intentional change to active entitlements is the explicit future disruption policy, §12). `ck_price_quote_option_kind` gains `membership`. **No `EntitlementPurchase` id exists on the quote — the Purchase does not exist at quote time.**

**Reservation quote authorization (Correction A2 — at quote creation, all server-side):** authenticated account owns the beneficiary participant (certified check) · the entitlement belongs to that participant/account/org/program (structural via the five-column FK; cross-account/cross-participant entitlement ids are NOT-FOUND-shaped) · the entitlement exists ⇒ its purchase is confirmed; effective validity currently permits reservation · the purchased snapshot allows reservation (`reservation_required` true; walk-in-only products do not reserve) · the requested occurrence is permitted by the purchased terms (branch limitation; snapshotted schedule pattern where schedule-bound; Program lineage) · a finite entitlement is not exhausted, with `availableToReserve` as a PROJECTION at quote time — the atomic authority is reservation CONFIRMATION (§7), because another device may commit the final available use after the quote is issued.

**Existing S5 rows valid with zero rewrites:** every existing row takes the default `capacityPurchase`, has exactly one unit, has a NULL revision, and carries a capacity option kind (no `package` quote was ever creatable — no purchasable package path existed), so both new CHECKs pass without touching a row.

**`requestQuote` dispatch:** by the price option's kind. Capacity kinds → the certified path unchanged (unit required and validated, session overrides, D-6 TTL). Entitlement kinds → any supplied unit is refused (`unitNotApplicable` typed outcome); an ACTIVE fulfillment revision is required — absent ⇒ fail-closed `fulfillmentUnavailable` (the `commissionTermsUnavailable`/`policyUnavailable` precedent); participant-ownership and age eligibility run identically (program-level rules; there is no unit to override them); money stays server-authored identically (base line from the option's `amount_fils`, offer application, deferred total = Σ lines); TTL and append-only immutability unchanged.

**Cross-trail impossibility (both directions, structural):**
- *An entitlement quote can never attach to a capacity Booking:* new uniques `uq_price_quote_id_session/(camp_week)/(cohort) UNIQUE (id, <unit>_id)` become targets for THREE additive composite FKs on `capacity_hold` — `fk_capacity_hold_quote_session FOREIGN KEY (quote_id, session_id) REFERENCES price_quote (id, session_id)` (and camp-week/cohort twins; the certified `fk_booking_hold_*` MATCH SIMPLE null-skip pattern). Consequence: a hold's unit must EQUAL its quote's unit (an additive strengthening of what `claimHold` already enforced in service code), and a zero-unit `entitlementAcquisition` quote can never be held — and `booking.hold_id NOT NULL` means never booked. Existing hold rows satisfy the FKs (the service always enforced unit=quote-unit; the `0017` migration asserts it).
- *A capacity quote can never attach to an EntitlementPurchase:* `entitlement_purchase.quote_id` carries `uq_entitlement_purchase_quote UNIQUE (quote_id)` (one purchase per quote, forever — retries rejoin by idempotency snapshot) and a BEFORE INSERT trigger **`enforce_entitlement_purchase_shape()`** (the `enforce_payment_intent_amount` precedent): the referenced quote must have `commercial_shape = 'entitlementAcquisition'` and matching `account_id`/`participant_id`/`organization_id`/`program_id`/`price_option_id`/`offer_id` — a capacity or reservation quote is refused at the row level.
- *A reservation quote* feeds only the certified hold→Booking flow (its shape permits holds), is refused by the purchase trigger, and can never gain a PaymentIntent (`enforce_payment_intent_amount` refuses zero totals).

**Commercial-target continuity — the two trails, no crossing:**
`capacityPurchase quote → CapacityHold → Booking → PaymentIntent(target=Booking) → W5 trust/ledger → confirmPaidBooking` (unchanged), and
`entitlementAcquisition quote → EntitlementPurchase → PaymentIntent(target=EntitlementPurchase) → the SAME W5 trust/ledger machinery → confirmPaidEntitlementPurchase → Entitlement`.

### 5.4 `confirmPaidEntitlementPurchase` — the entitlement success seam, and the exact V1 late-success rule (Correction 4)

Invoked ONLY by the W5-4 saga (never imported by any `http/` module; same source-lock family as `confirmPaidBooking`); `runIdempotent` scope `entitlement.confirm.paid`, `principalRef 'system:payments'`. **Binding rule: payment success never grants permission to violate an entitlement-domain invariant.** At trusted financial success (verified signed evidence + provider inspection corroborating captured amount — the certified steps, unchanged), under the purchase row lock:

**CONFIRM** iff ALL hold: (1) the purchase exists and is in `pending_payment`/`payment_failed` (an already-`confirmed` purchase replays its snapshot — converged, no duplicate entitlement: `entitlement.purchase_id UNIQUE`); (2) reciprocal identity — `intent.purchase_id/quote_id/account_id` match the purchase row (composite FK + runtime `reciprocalMismatch` check, the `confirmPaidBooking` discipline); (3) the participant target is still valid under canonical acquisition rules — the participant row is `active` and still belongs to the purchasing account; (4) the quote/revision snapshot is intact (structurally guaranteed by immutability; fail-closed if violated). A purchase whose `expires_at` has passed but which is still non-terminal CONFIRMS on genuine capture — there is no scarce-inventory race, and the window is abandonment metadata (§5.1), not capacity ownership.

**COMPENSATE** (same-amount `reversal` through the existing machinery; purchase → `compensated`; no entitlement; no settleable commission) iff any certified invariant refuses fulfillment: the purchase is already TERMINAL (`expired` — swept before capture; terminals are never resurrected, the W5 discipline) · the participant is archived or no longer belongs to the account · reciprocal/commercial-target mismatch or duplicate-target anomaly · any entitlement-domain invariant failure at creation. **Deliberately NOT in the refusal set (no invented semantics):** program/option/provider state changes after quote — no canonical rule treats them as blocking already-started purchases, and the immutable revision means later edits cannot alter what was sold; a future provider-revocation/disruption policy would be its own owner-ruled feature.

### 5.5 Zero-price entitlement acquisition — the non-payment boundary (Correction B), and commission

A provider may genuinely offer a free entitlement product (e.g., a free introductory 3-use package). Its acquisition NEVER creates payment state — no PaymentIntent, no Stripe Checkout, no payment transaction, no economics row, no observable `pending_payment` — merely because paid acquisition uses `EntitlementPurchase`. The zero-price analogue of the certified S5 rule is **`confirmFreeEntitlementPurchase`** (`runIdempotent` scope `entitlement.confirm.free`, customer principal), which in ONE transaction: validates the fresh authoritative `entitlementAcquisition` quote (shape guard + TTL + `total_fils = 0` — a nonzero quote is refused `notFreeQuote`) · validates participant/account ownership and participant activeness · validates the quote's immutable fulfillment revision · creates exactly one `EntitlementPurchase` inserted AS `confirmed` (§5.1) · creates exactly one `Entitlement` with validity snapshotted from this acquisition confirmation (D-S6-4) · emits the audit/outbox set exactly once · replays its snapshot on any same-key retry. The certified zero-total trigger independently refuses any PaymentIntent for such a quote — **zero money never enters the payment stack (D-W5 behavior preserved), and no commission/settlement event arises from nonexistent money**. D-10 `freeTrial` is not repurposed.

**Idempotency + quote single-use (Corrections B1/B2):** a same-key storm on free acquisition yields exactly one Purchase, one Entitlement, one audit/outbox effect set; an ambiguous-transport retry with the same key returns the same snapshot (proof 19). One `entitlementAcquisition` quote can never yield multiple confirmed Purchases/Entitlements through double-click, concurrent requests, free-path retry, paid-path retry, or a free/paid cross-path attempt: `uq_entitlement_purchase_quote UNIQUE (quote_id)` is the structural authority (both the free boundary and paid initiation INSERT the purchase row referencing the quote — the second path, whichever it is, collides and is refused with a typed outcome or replays its own idempotent snapshot; proof 20).

**Commission (unchanged D-W5-7):** the generalized `startPaidCheckout` snapshots economics once at PAID initiation — AED 1,000 at the 1200-bps launch term ⇒ 120/880, immutable; **a package/membership earns commission exactly once, when sold**; redemption/attendance touches no payment table and emits no commission event; payouts remain future work.

## 7. Reservation against finite entitlements — commitment semantics (Correction 5)

`reservation_required` / `walk_in_allowed` stay independent product booleans (§3). **Walk-in use:** credential straight against the entitlement (§9). **Reservation-required use:** the customer reserves a future eligible occurrence via the CERTIFIED capacity flow — an `entitlementReservation` quote (§4: unit-bound, zero-total, ENTITLEMENT-bound) → hold → Booking — plus the S6 subtype **`entitlement_reservation (booking_id PK → booking, entitlement_id → entitlement, organization_id org-composite, created_at)`** created in the same confirming transaction. **No second capacity system exists** — Session scarcity stays entirely S5's.

**`confirmEntitlementReservation` — the dedicated reservation confirmation authority (Correction A3).** An `entitlementReservation` quote does NOT fall through `confirmFreeBooking` as if it were a provider's free activity. The command is a distinct internal domain boundary (`runIdempotent` scope `entitlement.reservation.confirm`, customer principal) whose authority REQUIRES the Entitlement as a first-class input; it reuses certified S5 primitives internally (hold validation, `confirmCore` counter/consumption mechanics) and in ONE transaction establishes: quote still valid, `entitlementReservation`-shaped, and quote.entitlement_id ≡ the commanded entitlement · account/participant ownership (certified checks + the §4 five-column FK) · the exact entitlement effectively active and reservable under its PURCHASED snapshot · **`availableToReserve > 0` under the entitlement lock** (the atomic authority — quote-time projections grant nothing) · capacity-unit availability via the live hold (certified truth) · the capacity Booking + the `entitlement_reservation` subtype row · audit/outbox exactly once. **No PaymentIntent. No Stripe. No attendance. No usage consumption.**

**Canonical lock hierarchy (round-3 FINAL — stated in terms of actual contested ROW locks; `entitlement_reservation` is NOT a lock-order stage):**

> **idempotency key → capacity unit → hold → booking → credential → entitlement**

Every command takes an order-compatible subset of that hierarchy; MUTATIONS are ordered separately, after the relevant locks:

- **Reservation creation:** locks `key → unit FOR UPDATE → hold → booking → entitlement FOR UPDATE`; **then, with the Entitlement lock HELD: recompute the authoritative finite commitment state, verify `availableToReserve > 0`, and only then INSERT the `entitlement_reservation` commitment**, then audit/outbox, then commit. The persistent commitment row is NEVER inserted before the Entitlement lock is acquired — correctness never depends on transaction-snapshot or self-row visibility of provisional commitment rows. The Booking/capacity mutations earlier in the same transaction are provisional and roll back atomically when the Entitlement has no reservable use.
- **Walk-in redemption:** locks `key → credential FOR UPDATE → entitlement FOR UPDATE`; then the attendance INSERT + credential consumption CAS.
- **Reserved-use redemption:** the Booking row needs NO lock — check-in mutates only the credential and inserts attendance (the Booking's state is untouched in V1), so the reservation/Booking/occurrence rows are VALIDATED BY READ under the already-serialized credential + entitlement locks; the sequence stays `key → credential → entitlement` and no path anywhere takes `credential → booking`, so no reverse-Booking-lock hazard exists.
- **Release / occurrence conclusion:** the derived-commitment model needs no Entitlement lock (statuses derive from committed truth, §7 lifecycle); hold release/expiry and unit sweeps keep their certified S5 lock orders and never touch entitlement locks. Any FUTURE release mutation that genuinely needs both rows must take `booking → entitlement`, never the reverse.

**Isolation requirement (binding on S6-3 implementation):** under the project's READ COMMITTED model, the commitment-count query MUST execute after the Entitlement row lock has actually been acquired, as a subsequent statement with a fresh statement snapshot — a transaction that waited on the Entitlement lock thereby observes the prior holder's committed reservation; the design must never fold the availability read into a pre-wait stale snapshot.

**Final-credit two-transaction proof (pinned as a real PostgreSQL multi-connection proof in S6-3 — proof 11):** entitlement `totalUses = 1`; T1 and T2 concurrently reserve two DIFFERENT Sessions. Both independently acquire their different capacity-unit/hold/booking locks. At the shared Entitlement: T1 acquires the Entitlement lock, recomputes `used + reservedUpcoming` fresh, sees one reservable use, inserts its reservation, commits; T2 then acquires the same lock, performs a FRESH authoritative commitment query after lock acquisition, observes T1's committed reservation, computes `availableToReserve = 0`, refuses (`entitlementFullyCommitted`), and rolls its provisional Booking/capacity mutations back atomically. Result: exactly one reservation exists and exactly one capacity Booking commits — never by depending on seeing an uncommitted reservation. Proof 21 exercises the full reserve/release/check-in/expiry interleaving matrix against this hierarchy.

**Structural non-crossing (Correction A4 — no zero-price escape hatch around commitment accounting):** `confirmFreeBooking` gains a shape guard refusing any quote with `commercial_shape ≠ 'capacityPurchase'` (bounded owning-slice amendment; every historical quote IS `capacityPurchase`, so certified behavior is unchanged) — an `entitlementReservation` quote submitted there is refused (proof 15) · `confirmEntitlementReservation` refuses any quote with `commercial_shape ≠ 'entitlementReservation'` — a normal zero-price capacity quote cannot enter it (proof 14) · an acquisition quote can never become a reservation and vice versa (`ck_price_quote_shape` — shapes are insert-frozen on the append-only quote) · a reservation quote can never create an `EntitlementPurchase` (the §4 purchase shape trigger) and never a PaymentIntent (zero-total trigger) · one reservation binds exactly one Entitlement and one Booking/occurrence (`entitlement_reservation.booking_id` PK + single `entitlement_id` + the Booking's certified exactly-one-unit) · knowing another customer's Entitlement id conveys NO authority (five-column ownership FK + not-found shaping; proofs 17–18).

**Commitment vocabulary (owner-stated, adopted):** `used` = successfully redeemed attendance (append-only count). `reserved/committed` = a finite entitlement unit temporarily allocated to a future scheduled use, not yet consumed. `remaining` (historical) = `uses_total − used`. `availableToReserve` = `uses_total − used − activeFiniteReservations`. **A reserved credit is never mislabeled attended.**

**Reservation lifecycle — stored truth vs derived status (Correction C; no mutable duplicated status column exists):** STORED truth is exactly: the append-only `entitlement_reservation` row · the Booking's certified state · the occurrence's times on its unit row · append-only `attendance_record`. From those four, the lifecycle statuses are DERIVED deterministically — **`reservedUpcoming`** (Booking `confirmed` ∧ occurrence not concluded ∧ no attendance) · **`fulfilled`** (attendance record exists) · **`released`** (Booking `cancelled_by_customer`/`cancelled_by_provider`, where such authority exists) · **`concludedNoShow`** (Booking `confirmed` ∧ occurrence concluded ∧ no attendance — consumes nothing in V1). `availableToReserve` therefore never depends on frontend interpretation: **`activeFiniteReservations`** = COUNT of reservations in `reservedUpcoming`. Consequences, each an owner-required invariant: check-in against a reservation atomically converts commitment → consumption (the single attendance INSERT flips the row from the reserved-count into the used-count — atomic by construction); cancellation of a reservation Booking (any future cancellation authority) restores availability derivationally; **a no-show does not consume the use in V1** — a concluded occurrence with no attendance leaves `used` unchanged and drops out of the commitment count (availability restored derivationally; no-show release *policy/penalties* stay deferred with the owner, and the schema forces no consumption).

**Concurrency (owner example: 1 Pilates use remaining must not be reservable Mon+Wed+Fri):** reservation creation computes `availableToReserve` under the **entitlement row lock** and refuses at ≤ 0 (`entitlementFullyCommitted` typed outcome); two sessions racing the final available unit → exactly one reservation succeeds (proof 11, §17); the structural backstop invariant is *active commitments + used can never exceed `uses_total`*. **Lock ordering:** governed by the canonical round-3 hierarchy below — the entitlement lock is always last, the commitment insert happens under it, and no S6 transaction ever takes a unit/hold/booking/credential lock after an entitlement lock (no new deadlock or oversell path).
