# 01 — Product Context

## 1. Product name

**Himma** is the current product name.

The company identity and visual branding are provisional. A professional branding team may later replace or refine the brand system without changing the product structure or customer experience.

## 2. One-sentence definition

Himma is a UAE consumer mobile marketplace where individuals and families discover, compare, book, pay for, gift, and manage activities from verified providers in one place.

## 3. The problem Himma solves

Activities in the UAE are fragmented.

Customers often discover activities through:

- Instagram
- TikTok
- Google
- WhatsApp
- Friends and family
- Individual provider websites
- Flyers
- Separate gym or studio applications
- Phone calls

The customer then has to determine:

- Which providers exist nearby
- What each provider actually offers
- Whether the activity is suitable
- The schedule
- The price
- Whether a trial exists
- Whether places are available
- Whether a session is ladies only, mixed, boys only, girls only, or otherwise restricted
- How to pay
- How to remember and manage bookings across several providers

Himma consolidates this fragmented journey into one customer product.

## 4. The two primary consumer needs

### 4.1 Individuals managing their own active life

Adults and eligible older users may want several activities from different providers.

A customer may have:

- Pilates at one studio
- Kickboxing at another gym
- Calisthenics at another facility
- Padel on certain evenings
- Swimming at another club
- Massage or sports recovery at another provider
- A language, Quran, art, music, or technology class elsewhere

Today, these activities can be difficult to discover and harder to manage together.

Himma helps the individual:

- Discover what is available nearby
- Browse activities visually
- Search directly when they know what they want
- Compare programs and providers
- See schedules, pricing, eligibility, and availability
- Book and pay inside Himma
- Manage all bookings in one schedule
- Use credits and rewards
- Gift an activity or credit to a friend
- Add confirmed bookings to a preferred external calendar
- Receive personalized recommendations based on interests and history

### 4.2 Parents building beneficial routines for children and young people

Himma is also designed for parents and guardians who want children and young people to spend more time in structured, beneficial activities.

This is not a children's entertainment app focused only on toys, cartoons, or simple play.

The product should help families discover activities that can support:

- Physical activity
- Fitness and healthy routines
- Mental development
- Learning
- Discipline
- Confidence
- Social interaction
- Creativity
- Skill progression
- Productive after-school time
- Productive weekends and school holidays
- Reduced passive screen time

Relevant activities may include:

- Football
- Swimming
- Boxing and martial arts
- Gymnastics
- Padel and other sports
- Quran reading, memorization, and Tajweed
- Arabic and English learning
- Coding and robotics
- Art and music
- Public speaking
- Camps
- Outdoor activities
- Workshops
- Other body- and mind-development programs

A parent may manage several children with different ages, interests, schedules, and eligibility requirements.

## 5. Social purpose

Himma's social purpose is to reduce the friction between wanting a more active, beneficial lifestyle and actually participating in one.

For individuals, this means making physical, educational, creative, and wellness activities easier to discover and maintain.

For families, this means helping parents create structured routines that encourage children and young people to move, learn, create, develop skills, and spend less unstructured time in passive digital consumption.

Himma is an enabling marketplace, not a medical service. The product must not make unsupported claims that using the app will treat or prevent a specific disease.

## 6. Himma owns the complete customer journey

Himma is not a directory or lead-generation page.

The intended customer journey is:

1. Discover an activity, program, provider, collection, or offer.
2. Compare relevant providers and programs.
3. View schedule, price, location, eligibility, policies, reviews, and availability.
4. Select the participating person or people.
5. Select a session, membership, package, term, camp, or trial.
6. Book inside Himma.
7. Pay inside Himma.
8. Receive confirmation inside Himma.
9. Manage the booking and schedule inside Himma.
10. Add the confirmed activity to an external calendar if desired.
11. Receive updates, cancellations, credits, refunds, rewards, and reminders through Himma.
12. Leave a verified review after an eligible completed booking.

The Himma booking record is intended to become the source of truth.

## 7. Geographic direction

The initial operating focus is Abu Dhabi, UAE.

The product should use realistic Abu Dhabi areas and behavior in mock data, while keeping the future data model suitable for wider UAE expansion.

Examples include:

- Khalifa City
- Yas Island
- Al Reem Island
- Al Raha
- Saadiyat Island
- Abu Dhabi Island
- Mohammed Bin Zayed City

## 8. Provider ecosystem context

Providers will eventually receive a separate partner experience.

The future provider flow is:

1. A provider expresses interest through a public partnership page.
2. Himma reviews and verifies the provider.
3. Approved provider users receive access to a provider web portal.
4. The provider manages its storefront, branches, programs, sessions, schedules, capacity, pricing, media, policies, and bookings.
5. Approved provider data appears in the customer mobile app.
6. Himma administrators control verification, sensitive changes, support, finance, categories, and moderation through a separate admin portal.

The provider and admin products are important context, but they are not part of the current customer mobile frontend build.

## 9. Future business expansion

A future product may serve:

- Schools
- Universities
- Companies
- Government organizations
- Nurseries
- Other institutions

That future product may include group trips, quotations, invoices, purchase orders, institutional approvals, and large-group workflows.

It is intentionally deferred so the first Himma product remains focused on individuals and families.

No school, university, company, organization, quotation, purchase-order, or group-trip workflow should appear in the current customer mobile app.
